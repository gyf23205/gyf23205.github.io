This work uses Koopman operator theory to decompose neural network controllers into reusable modules with theoretical guarantees, so that learned policies are preserved while new ones are acquired. It enables rapid policy transfer across tasks, environments, and embodiments on quadruped robots and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), in simulation and real-world experiments, with up to 56% better convergence and 81% better jump-start than baselines.
